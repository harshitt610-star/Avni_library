Live Room Golden Rules

StoryNest Kid-Safe Standards

1
Be Kind & Encouraging

Every story idea is valid. We applaud peers when they share their draft characters.

2
Virtual Hand for Speaking

Click the hand icon in Google Meet before unmuting so our mentor can invite everyone fairly.

3
Chat for Story Ideas Only

Keep comments focused on workshop questions, character names, and creative prompts.

4
Dedicated Moderator Present

A verified StoryNest staff monitor is inside every session to assist children and ensure peace.
