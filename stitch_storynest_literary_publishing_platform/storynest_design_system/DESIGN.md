## Brand & Style

This design system navigates a dual-personality ecosystem: a joyful, secure, and encouraging sanctuary for young readers, parents, and educators, counterbalanced by a sophisticated, credible editorial workshop for independent authors and publishers. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Editorial Tactile Warmth**—marrying the tangible comfort of printed picture books and physical paper stock with the clean precision of modern creator tools. 

- **Audience & Archetypes:**
  - *Learners & Guardians:* Welcomed by comforting paper tones, friendly rounded modules, encouraging star counters, and soft tactile elements that eliminate visual intimidation and reduce eye strain.
  - *Educators & Authors:* Grounded by refined typographic hierarchy, purposeful whitespace, ink-driven contrast, and focused workspace utilities.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, curiosity, literary respect, creative agency, and unimpeachable trust.
- **Visual Stance:** Avoids clinical, sterile white-canvas software tropes in favor of a warm, parchment-tinted realm where ink, paper, forest greenery, and vibrant craft accents harmoniously coexist.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic feeling of bound books, organic forest discovery, and vibrant editorial marginalia.

- **Primary (`#203047` - Deep Ink Navy):** The core stabilizing tone. Used for authoritative typography, primary navigation headers, high-emphasis structural boundaries, and default elevated actions.
- **Secondary (`#2F6B57` - Forest Green):** Represents educational growth, literacy milestones, progress meters, and author publishing metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#E9B44C` - Golden Amber):** The beacon of celebration. Deployed for reading streaks, reward badges, workshop status badges, and star ratings.
- **Accent Coral (`#E98972`):** Reserved for energetic interactive touchpoints, juvenile discovery CTAs, and playful prompts.
- **Backgrounds & Containers:**
  - *Canvas Ground (`#FAF7F0` - Warm Cream):* Default reading and layout surface, providing non-glare, paper-like optical comfort.
  - *Surface Low (`#E7EFE8` - Soft Sage):* Calming container background for child-friendly panels, classroom hubs, and subtle groupings.
  - *Surface Card (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):* High-clarity sheet layer for active reading cards, content tiles, and authoring modules.
- **Text & Semantics:**
  - *Primary Body (`#263238`):* Off-black slate ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against cream surfaces.
  - *Secondary Text (`#667085`):* Muted slate for metadata, chapter captions, and attribution labels.
  - *Border Subtlety (`#E6E7E5`):* Organic boundary stroke for light card definitions.
  - *Feedback:* Success (`#247A52`), Error (`#B42318`), Warning (`#B54708`).

## Typography

The type system balances long-form narrative cadence with utilitarian application clarity.

- **Primary Display & Literary Voice (`Merriweather`):** Applied to book titles, editorial feature heads, reading experiences, and section banners. Provides a warm, open x-height that welcomes young readers and signals literary distinction to authors. 
- **Application & UI Voice (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied to dashboards, metadata, controls, buttons, publishing tables, and annotations. Its gentle grotesque curves keep the interface approachable without sacrificing operational density.
- **Multilingual & Script Fallback:** For cross-platform bilingual publishing encompassing Devanagari (Hindi) and Latin characters, ensure fonts pair natively with systemic fallbacks:
  `font-family: "Merriweather", "Noto Serif Devanagari", Georgia, serif;` for editorial narrative headers, and 
  `font-family: "Plus Jakarta Sans", "Noto Sans Devanagari", system-ui, sans-serif;` for functional UI components.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a rhythmic, responsive 12-column fluid grid that adapts dynamically based on workflow:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum reading viewports are constrained to `720px` to maintain an ideal 65-75 character line length. Publishing analytics and author workspaces expand up to `1320px` max-width.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Split-screen reading and activity modules reflow into stacked cards.
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Two-column book galleries shift into single-column or horizontally scrolling carousels.
- **Layout Rhythm:** Component spacing strictly adheres to an 8pt base grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) encapsulates module transitions to avoid cognitive overload for children and maintain editorial breathing room for adults.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tactile Ambient Warmth** rather than cold synthetic dropshadows. Depth mimics physical sheets of textured paper laid upon a flat wooden desk:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Canvas background `#FAF7F0` and inset workbenches.
- **Level 1 (Paper Surface):** Raised card layer (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #E6E7E5`) accompanied by an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(32, 48, 71, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(32, 48, 71, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating & Interactive):** Interactive elements under hover, book preview modals, and active child tasks: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(32, 48, 71, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(32, 48, 71, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Reader drawers, chapter quick-selectors, manuscript upload modals: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(32, 48, 71, 0.12)`.
- **Tactile Button Press:** Interactive controls depress visually under active clicks, dropping their Y-axis translation by `1px` to reinforce physical feedback.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a contextual shape taxonomy:

- **Playful / Child-Facing Surfaces:** Use expansive curvature (`rounded-2xl` / `1.25rem` to `1.5rem`) on badges, cover cards, achievement banners, and activity panels to eliminate sharp visual friction and impart safety.
- **Author & Management Surfaces:** Utilize structural, focused curvature (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` to `rounded-xl` / `0.75rem`) across data tables, editor panes, input groups, and analytics cards to maximize information density.
- **Chips, Badges, & Action Pills:** Styled fully rounded (`9999px`) for high tactile grabability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Navy:** Background `#203047`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.75rem`. Hover shifts to `#162233` with Level 2 elevation.
- **Interactive Action / Discovery (Coral):** Background `#E98972`, text `#FFFFFF`. High-energy CTA for read-along launches and story publishing actions. Hover transitions to `#DC755C`.
- **Secondary Sage:** Background `#E7EFE8`, text `#2F6B57`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover shifts to `#D6E3D8`.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum height of `48px` on child surfaces and mobile viewports for accessible tap areas.

### Cards
- **Story / Book Card:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` subtle outline in `#E6E7E5`, `rounded-2xl` corners. Aspect ratio for cover media is fixed at 3:4. Includes internal padding of `space-md` with Level 1 warm shadow. Hover subtly scales cover imagery by 1.02.
- **Author Analytics Card:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl`, subtle border, housing data-dense metrics with forest-green progress badges.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, padding `0 12px`.
- **Reading Milestone:** Amber surface (`rgba(233, 180, 76, 0.18)`), text `#8D6513`, icon adorned with Golden Amber `#E9B44C`.
- **Publishing Status (Draft / Review / Published):** Sage and Forest Green variants utilizing semantic indicators.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E6E7E5`, border-radius `0.75rem`, text `#263238`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus State:** High-contrast double-ring: inner stroke `#203047` with an outer diffuse glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(32, 48, 71, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px x 20px` footprint. Border `2px solid #203047`. Selected state filled with `#203047` containing a white checkmark. Child-accessible alternate version scales to `24px x 24px` with `#2F6B57` filling.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Reading Progress Bar:** Track background `#E7EFE8`, filled indicator `#2F6B57`, height `8px`, `rounded-full`. In story reader view, displays an embedded golden star marker at chapter intervals.
- **Bilingual Language Switcher:** Dual pill-toggle switch (`English` / `हिंदी`) utilizing `#203047` for active language badge with zero-latency font swap.
